6 climates, and 3 hours between them

Essaouira never leaves the teens. Merzouga passes forty. Ifrane goes below zero and takes more rain than London. All three are in the same country, and you can drive between any two of them in a day.

What each shape is telling you

PlaceThe shape
EssaouiraAlmost flat. The cold Atlantic upwelling holds the whole year inside about ten degrees, summer and winter alike
MarrakechA steep summer arch on very little rain. Three hours from Essaouira and twenty degrees hotter in July
TangierMediterranean: mild, and genuinely wet in winter. More rain than Marrakech gets in four years
FesContinental. Cold nights in January and forty in August, because there is no sea moderating it
IfraneBelow freezing, with snow. Altitude does what latitude cannot
MerzougaThe widest daily swing and almost no rain at all. The desert is defined by the second of those, not the first

The three things that set it

The ocean
Cold upwelling water flattens the coastal year completely
The Atlas
A wall that takes the rain out of the air and leaves the far side dry
Altitude
About six degrees cooler for every thousand metres up

The Atlas is the decisive one. Wet Atlantic air is forced up the northern slope, cools, and drops its water on the way. What crosses the ridge has nothing left, which is why Ifrane takes over eight hundred millimetres and Merzouga, on the other side, takes fifty.

That single mechanism explains the cedar forest, the oases, the khettara, the argan belt and the date palm all at once. Every one of them is positioned by where the rain stops.

What it means for a visitor

WhenWhere it works
December to FebruaryMarrakech and the south are pleasant by day and cold at night. The Atlas has snow. The coast is wet in the north
March to MayThe best of it almost everywhere. Rose harvest, green Atlas, and the desert still bearable
June to AugustThe coast, and only the coast. Essaouira and Agadir are twenty degrees below Marrakech
September to NovemberThe desert reopens, the dates come in, and the light turns

A house in the medina is designed for the July figure, not the January one. That is why it feels cold in winter and works perfectly in August, and why the calendar of light module and this one describe the same building from two directions.
