Most kitchens have no oven

A gas ring for tagines, a kettle, and that is the room. The bread goes out to the ferran, comes back at midday, and the baker sorts thirty families' loaves by the marks pressed into them.

Eight breads

Each one is defined by where it is cooked, not by what is in it.

Khobz
Ferran, wood-fired
The daily loaf. Round, slightly flattened, pricked with a fork before it goes in.
Batbout
Pan, no oven
Puffs into a dome, collapses as it cools, and the collapse leaves the pocket.
Msemen
Griddle
Folded square, roughly forty layers of dough with butter trapped between them.
Meloui
Griddle
Same dough as msemen, wound into a coil instead of folded flat.
Rghaif
Griddle
Smaller and crisper than msemen, often stuffed with kefta or vegetables.
Baghrir
Griddle, one side only
Bubbles rise and burst, leaving holes that stay open. Judged by the count.
Harcha
Griddle, dry
Coarse semolina, no yeast. Crumbles rather than tears.
Medfouna
Buried in ash
Rissani. Filling sealed between two discs. The name means buried.

Sixty per cent of the wheat is imported

Domestic production tracks rainfall and nothing else. A good year yields around ten million tonnes and a drought year roughly half that, so the shortfall is bought abroad at whatever the world price happens to be.

Flour is a political instrument

The Caisse de Compensation subsidises flour to hold the price of a loaf steady. Bread riots are not a metaphor in this part of the world, and successive governments have treated the subsidy as untouchable.

The five things a quarter has

A Moroccan neighbourhood is defined by five shared institutions. The oven is one of them, which is why losing it changes the shape of the quarter rather than just the shopping.

The same breads, other countries

Nothing on this list stops at the Moroccan border. The griddle breads are Maghrebi and the communal oven is Mediterranean, which is what happens when a food follows a technique rather than a flag.

BreadWhere else
BaghrirAlgeria and Tunisia make it too. Amazigh in origin, which is why it crosses three borders without changing name.
