65,000 cooperatives, 789,000 members

Morocco has organised its producers unusually well: sixty-five thousand cooperatives, nearly eight hundred thousand members, and a national authority running since 1962. The opportunity in front of the sector now is distribution — reaching buyers who are not standing in front of it.

Updated 7 September 2026 ODCO 2026 registrations; craft and payment figures from 2023–2025 reporting.

The room to grow

The production side is dense and formalised. The online side is young: the largest domestic marketplace runs about seven hundred vendors, and 83.8% of Moroccan online buyers still settle in cash on delivery. Every one of those figures is a market that has not been built yet.

What that costs

Handicrafts are about 7% of GDP and employ 2.7 million people, on a turnover of 140 billion dirhams. Exports hit a record 1.1 billion dirhams in 2023 and are rising. The domestic market has carried the sector this far on its own.

The argan case shows the scale available. Rural women hold a UNESCO-inscribed practice and supply a global cosmetic market worth more than seven hundred million dollars.

Toudarte’s plan is worth noticing. Rather than moving the oil to the customer, they are bringing the customer to the forest. That is a distribution strategy dressed as hospitality, and it is one any cooperative could copy.
