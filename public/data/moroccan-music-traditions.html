6 traditions, and none of them share a language

Moroccan music is not a genre with regional variants. It is six separate systems that grew in different places for different reasons, sung in classical Arabic, Darija, Tashelhit, Tamazight, Hassaniya and words carried north from Mali. They coexist without merging.

Six languages, and the language tells you the tradition

The useful test is what the audience does. At an Andalusi concert they listen. At a malhun evening they take the refrain. At an ahwash there is no audience at all, because everybody is in the circle. At a Gnawa lila the music is doing something to somebody, and watching is beside the point.

The three instruments that are not from here

InstrumentWhere it came from
OudThe Arab east, by way of al-Andalus. Ziryab is credited with adding a fifth course to it in ninth-century Córdoba
GuembriThe Sahel, carried north with the people who were brought north. Three strings, a camel-skin face, a body of poplar
KamanjaThe European violin, adopted whole and then held upright on the knee, which changes how it is fingered and what it can do

The rabab and the bendir are the ones that were always here. Almost everything else arrived, and the arrival routes are the same ones in the trans-Saharan and al-Andalus modules: north across the desert, and west across the strait.

The line and the circle

Ahwash and ahidous are often described as the same thing. They are not. Ahwash, in the Souss and the Anti-Atlas, is danced in a circle or a crescent with the drummers at the centre, and the singing is antiphonal between groups.

Ahidous, in the Middle Atlas, is danced in a line, shoulder to shoulder, men and women alternating, with a shoulder movement that runs down the row like a wave. Both use the bendir and almost nothing else. What differs is the geometry, and the geometry is the tradition.

What everybody actually listens to

FormWhere it sits
ChaabiThe popular music of the country. Weddings, taxis, cafés. Absorbs everything above it and puts it over a fast rhythm
RaiCross-border from Oran, and enormous in the east
Moroccan hip-hopFrom Casablanca in the early 2000s. Darija over boom-bap, and now the largest audience of anything on this page
FusionNass El Ghiwane from 1970 onward, who took Gnawa and Aissawa material into popular song and changed what was possible

Nass El Ghiwane are the hinge. Before them the six traditions and popular music were separate worlds. After them, a guembri in a pop song was ordinary, and every Moroccan band since has had that door open to it.
