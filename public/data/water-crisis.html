Two thousand cubic metres, then six hundred

Renewable water per person in Morocco has fallen by roughly two thirds since 1960. The seven-year drought ended in January 2026 and the dams refilled, which changes this year and not the line.

Renewable water per person, cubic metres a year

Population growth and a hotter, drier climate, compounding. The United Nations classifies anything below 500 cubic metres as extreme water scarcity, and Morocco is projected to reach it around 2050.

Not only Morocco

The Mediterranean's southern rim is drying together. Morocco's dams refilled in 2026; the trend line underneath is regional and it does not respond to one good winter.

Morocco's answer is unusual in one respect. The Casablanca plant will be the largest in Africa powered entirely by renewable energy, which turns a water problem into an argument for the solar capacity the country already built.
