2 official languages, and a third that everybody speaks

In 2011 Morocco made Tamazight an official language of the state alongside Arabic, which few countries in the region have done. Darija, which 92% of the country speaks every day, holds no official status at all and runs the place regardless.

Where each one is

Tarifit in the Rif, Central Atlas Tamazight through the mountains, Tashelhit across the Souss and the Anti-Atlas, Hassaniya in the far south. Spanish survives in the north because the north was Spanish until 1956.

Speaking it and writing it are different things

Tamazight became an official language in 2011 and Tifinagh was adopted as its script in 2003. The 2024 census asked separately about reading and writing.

What Darija is made of

A speaker of Standard Arabic from the Gulf will not follow a conversation in Marrakech. The vocabulary explains why.

SourceWhat it contributed
ArabicThe grammatical spine and most of the core lexicon
AmazighVocabulary, place names, and a good deal of the phonology
FrenchAdministration, machinery, the modern world. Protectorate, 1912 to 1956
SpanishThe north and the kitchen. Cocina, semana, and the whole Rif coast

Darija is mostly spoken rather than written, though social media has been changing that for fifteen years. It has no official status, no standard orthography, and roughly thirty-four million speakers.
