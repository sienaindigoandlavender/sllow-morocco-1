47 degrees between June and December

At noon on the longest day the sun stands 82 degrees above Marrakech, almost overhead. At noon on the shortest it reaches 35. Every courtyard, every arcade and every deep-set window in the country is built around that one swing.

Updated 7 September 2026 Angles calculated for latitude 31.6° north. Add or subtract about a degree per 110 km.

The sun’s three paths

Three arcs, east to west: midsummer high overhead, the equinoxes in the middle, midwinter low and short. The gap between the top and bottom arcs is forty-seven degrees, which is twice the tilt of the earth and the same everywhere on the planet. What changes with latitude is where in the sky the whole set sits.

Which is what the courtyard is for

In summer the sun is so steep that it drops a small patch onto the middle of the courtyard floor around midday and touches nothing else. The rooms never see it. That is the whole design intention: a house with a hole in the middle is a house that is shaded all day in July.

In winter the same sun comes in at thirty-five degrees, clears the opposite roof and reaches deep into the north-facing rooms, warming exactly the wall that needs it. Nothing moves and nothing is adjusted. The building simply behaves differently because the sun does.

What follows from the angle

Deep-set
Windows recessed in thick wall. The reveal shades the glass in summer and not in winter
Arcades
A colonnade round a courtyard is a permanent parasol at a fixed depth
Narrow lanes
Streets built narrower than the buildings are tall are in shade for most of the day
Flat roofs
Usable because the sun is steep. A living floor for half the year

A medina lane is not narrow because land was expensive. It is narrow because a lane narrower than the walls are tall never gets direct sun on its floor, and walking through one in August is measurably cooler than standing in a square.

The light itself changes

SeasonWhat it does to a wall
Summer, high sunShort shadows, flat light, and the red of the pisé bleached toward pink. Nothing has relief between eleven and four
Winter, low sunLong raking shadows across the same wall. Every irregularity in the render shows, and the colour deepens toward brown
The two hours after dawnLow angle, warm colour, and the only time the whole medina has modelling in it
Dust in the airScatters the low sun further. The famous evening colour is partly the Haouz plain suspended in it

Which is why photographs of Marrakech taken in the middle of a summer day never look like the place. The colour everyone remembers only exists at a low sun angle, and for about four months of the year the sun is barely low at all until it is nearly gone.
