3 former finalists, on the way to the semi-final

At Qatar 2022 Morocco beat Belgium, Spain and Portugal and became the first African and first Arab nation to reach a World Cup semi-final. In 2030 the tournament comes here, a hundred years after the first one, shared with Spain and Portugal across two continents.

Updated 7 September 2026 Includes the completed 2026 tournament. The CAS ruling on the 2025 AFCON final is expected in October 2026.

The 2022 run

Three of the four teams Morocco eliminated or drew with in the knockout phase had reached a World Cup final before. The defence conceded once from open play across the entire tournament.

2026

Morocco were the first African side to qualify, with a perfect record in the CAF group, and came through Group C alongside Brazil. The run ended in the quarter-final against France, the same opponent that ended the 2022 run one round later.

The 2025 Africa Cup of Nations final, on the record

The outcome of that final is disputed and the dispute is unresolved. The sequence, without interpretation:

DateWhat is recorded
18 January 2026The final is played in Rabat. Senegal win 1–0 in extra time. The match is interrupted late in normal time when Senegal players leave the pitch after a penalty is awarded to Morocco; they return, and the penalty is missed
17 March 2026The CAF Appeal Board upholds an appeal by the Moroccan federation, applies Articles 82 and 84, and records the match as a 3–0 forfeit in Morocco’s favour
March 2026The Senegalese federation rejects the ruling and takes the matter to the Court of Arbitration for Sport

This module does not state a winner. The Court of Arbitration for Sport is expected to rule in October 2026, and this page will be updated when it does.
