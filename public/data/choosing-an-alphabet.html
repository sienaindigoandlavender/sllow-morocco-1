3 alphabets were on the table

A language with no agreed script cannot be taught, printed or put on a road sign. In 2003 Morocco had to choose one for Tamazight, and there were three candidates with three different constituencies behind them. The one that won is 2,500 years old.

Updated 7 September 2026 Implementation dates from IRCAM, the Ministry of Education and the 2024 Indigenous World report.

The same word, 3 ways

ⵜⴰⵎⴰⵣⵉⵖⵜ
Tifinagh
The indigenous script, descended from Libyco-Berber and kept alive by the Tuareg. Visually distinct from everything around it, which was the argument for it and against it.
Chosen, 2003
تامازيغت
Arabic
Favoured on the grounds of national unity and of an existing literate readership. Amazigh organisations read it as absorbing the language into an Arabic-Islamic frame.
Tamaziɣt
Latin
The script most Tamazight speakers were already writing in, and the one favoured by much of the cultural movement. Read by others as secular and Western-facing.

IRCAM recommended Tifinagh and the King approved it. It was a compromise between two politically loaded options, and it had the advantage of being neither. It also happens to be the script Numidian stonecutters were using before Rome arrived.

What has been built since

2001
IRCAM is founded
The Royal Institute of Amazigh Culture, by royal decree, with Mohamed Chafik as its first rector.
2003
Tifinagh adopted, and teaching begins
317 pilot primary schools, three hours a week, with a standardised curriculum built across the dialects.
2004
International recognition of the script
Which is what made it typeable, and therefore usable on a sign, a screen or a keyboard.
2008–09
A grammar, then a television channel
IRCAM publishes the reference grammar; the Amazigh channel goes on air.
2011
Article 5 of the constitution
Tamazight becomes an official language of the state alongside Arabic. Very few countries anywhere have done this for an indigenous language.
2018
Organic Law 26.16
Sets out how official status is to be implemented, and over what timetable.
2020
Into the courts
Tamazight is integrated into the judicial system in June.
2021
A full primary curriculum
Built with IRCAM and issued in January.
2023
Yennayer becomes a paid national holiday
Announced by the Royal Cabinet on 3 May. Amazigh New Year falls in mid-January.
2023
A Wikipedia of its own
Standard Moroccan Amazigh, language code zgh, accepted as an independent Wikipedia project in November.
2030
Target for teaching in every primary school
The rollout is sequenced from the pilot programme that began in 2003.

Twenty-five years from an institute with no agreed alphabet to a constitutionally official language with a script, a curriculum, a broadcaster and an encyclopaedia.
