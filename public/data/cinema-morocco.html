Morocco has played almost everywhere except Morocco

Tibet, Somalia, ancient Rome, Jerusalem, Egypt, Persia, Arrakis and Slaver’s Bay have all been filmed within a few hours of Ouarzazate. Films actually set in Morocco are the rare case.

Babel is the exception. Morocco played itself, and the Moroccan section was the one most of the reviews left out.

Where the sets are

Ouarzazate is the base. Everything else is within driving distance: kasbah, gorge, dune, ocean and medina inside a few hours of the same hotel.

The rebate arrived in 2018. Foreign productions put more than $100 million into the country in 2022, and in 2025 the CCM backed 23 foreign features generating over $165 million in local spend. Gladiator II carried a $200 million budget and left an estimated $30 million behind.

The domestic industry grew too

The incentive was designed to attract foreign shoots. It also built a crew base, and the crew base makes Moroccan films possible.

Ouarzazate keeps the shoot and loses the edit

Productions film here and finish elsewhere. Editing, visual effects and sound have always gone back to London, Paris or Los Angeles, which is the gap the new complex is meant to close.
