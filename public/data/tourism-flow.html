Two cities take two thirds of the nights

Morocco received 19.8 million visitors in 2025 and is aiming at 26 million by 2030. Marrakech and Agadir absorb around 65% of the overnight stays between them.

Compiled 7 September 2026 Latest figures: full year 2025 and first half of 2026.

Arrivals, millions

2020 is the border closure. Everything after it is recovery and then something beyond recovery: 2024 came in 35% above the pre-pandemic benchmark, and Morocco passed Egypt to become the most visited country in Africa.

Half of them are Moroccan

Moroccans living abroad count as arrivals, and they are close to half the total. The two halves behave differently: one books hotels, the other goes to family in August.

Receipts are rising faster than arrivals, which means the average visitor is spending more rather than the country simply receiving more people.

Against the neighbours

Morocco passed Egypt in 2024 to become the most visited country in Africa, and held the position in 2025. Both are a long way behind the northern shore of the same sea.

The 2030 target of 26 million is tied to co-hosting the World Cup with Spain and Portugal, which makes it the first tourism plan in Moroccan history built around an event happening on two continents at once.
