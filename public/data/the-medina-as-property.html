11,000 riads, and 1,500 of them are hotels

Inside the walls of the Marrakech medina there are roughly eleven thousand riads. About one in seven now operates as a guesthouse. The other six are still houses, and that ratio is the most important number nobody publishes.

The buyers are principally French, British, Belgian, Dutch, Swiss and American. Across Marrakech as a whole, foreign buyers and Moroccans living abroad account for something like 35 to 40% of all acquisitions; inside the medina the concentration is roughly double that.

3 words for a house

Riad
From the Arabic for gardens. Properly, a house built around a planted central patio, classically divided into four beds with a tree in each. Orange and lemon, usually.
Douria
Smaller, and without the garden. An open patio, called a houch, with the rooms around it. The commonest dwelling in the medina by a long way.
Dar
The large house or palace. Multiple patios, painted and carved cedar ceilings known as zouak, and floor areas that can pass 600 square metres.

Almost everything marketed as a riad is a douria. The distinction matters because the garden is the whole architectural idea: rooms facing inward onto water and shade, and a blank wall to the lane. A door that looks like nothing can open onto eight hundred square metres, which is not an accident of decay but the point of the design.

What restoration involves

The medina is a UNESCO-classified zone, so structural work is not a private matter.

RequirementDetail
AuthorisationEvery structural intervention requires approval from the Inspection des Monuments Historiques
TimescaleThree to six months minimum for permits, before any work starts
TradesSpecialist craftsmen for zellige, tadelakt and carved cedar. The apprenticeships behind those are eight to ten years
Acquisition costs8 to 10% on top of price: about 4% registration, 1.5 to 2% notary, 0.5 to 1% land registry
AccessDistance from vehicle access is a material factor. A house at the end of a lane no cart can reach is a different proposition from one five minutes from a car

That permit regime is why the conversions look the way they do. It is slow, it is expensive, and it is also the reason the fabric has been repaired in traditional materials rather than concrete.

What the pipeline says about 2030

Marrakech is a host city for the 2030 World Cup, and around 3,000 premium hotel rooms are reported to be in the pipeline against it. Transactions across the city rose roughly 12% between 2024 and 2026, at annual volumes of about eighteen thousand.

Which sets up the question the medina will answer over the next few years, and this module does not attempt to answer it: how many of the remaining nine and a half thousand houses stay houses.
